Your actions are the tools.

This is a turn-based time-loop puzzle: the room advances only when you move or wait. Walk to a switch, press R, then work alongside the recording of your previous self. Up to three echoes replay simultaneously and hold their final position.

Read the circuit. Each door and its control share a number. Amber plates accept the robot, an echo or a crate. Blue receivers accept only echoes. Purple switches latch when the live robot visits. Striped sockets require a crate. Keys must be collected by the live robot each run.

Plan, then rewind. Echoes replay crate pushes too. X restarts the current run; Remove Echo deletes the newest recording. Undo and the timeline let you revise the current run. Pulse doors open for two turns, then close for two; you can safely wait beside them.

No time pressure. Take as long as you need between turns. Every chamber is available from the selector. Progress saves on this browser. Reversing a saved echo is an optional experiment; it changes which endpoint the echo holds.

THE OCEAN HAS A FOOD CHAIN.

Climb it.

Begin as a tiny goldfish. Hunt smaller fish, escape larger predators, and evolve through eight species to become a megalodon. Hunt schools of small fish. Aim near a fish and click to lunge and bite; close prey are eaten automatically. Red rings mean danger.

Larger fish hunt the whole food chain. Hide behind coral reefs and seabed hills to break their line of sight. Your body grows much faster than your swim speed.

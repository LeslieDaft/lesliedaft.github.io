THE OCEAN HAS A FOOD CHAIN.

Choose your hunter.

Pick a juvenile species and keep it for your whole run. Every kill earns XP by the victim’s weight. Grow continuously, and level up for stronger bites and more health.

Left-click to bite. Damage alone gives no XP: finish the fish to claim its full weight reward. Only mouths can hurt you. Starfish on the seabed are easy first kills.

Ocean species & swimming detail

Goldfish, bluegill, bass, barracuda, reef shark, tiger shark, great white, megalodon, clownfish, blue tang, lionfish, pufferfish, angelfish, bluefin tuna, hammerhead, moray eel, swordfish, manta ray, parrotfish, catfish and seahorse. Full-body waves, moving fins, gills, patterned scales, eyes and bite jaws. Reef cover breaks a predator’s line of sight. Follow the ocean arrows and glowing current rings: each crossing is laid out for about 45 seconds at your arrival swimming speed. Mini keeps juvenile prey and titans beginning with megalodon; Normal runs megalodon to giant squid, Giant begins with giant squid, and Mythical ends with the immense 3,000 m Moby Dick. Growth and burst swimming shorten a crossing.

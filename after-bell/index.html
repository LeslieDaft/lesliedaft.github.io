After Bell

You suspect GoGuardian is hiding something. Stay after school, collect seven pieces of evidence, and uncover the fictional cult behind North Hollow. Each clue reveals another part of the mystery. Dawn arrives automatically; a missing clue means another night of investigating. Hold E to record, photograph, and print evidence, but every task can expose you. Teachers remember repeated hiding spots, nights grow more dangerous, and GoGuardian scans lockers and seals doors.
Take math/science and music theory tests during school: an A earns that teacher’s protection tonight, including a third-person rescue if they are nearby. Below 70% sends you to the principal and blocks protection on the next school day. Escape guarded detention with a key and fuse. On weekends, there are no teachers to save you: GoGuardian captures end your run.
Controls: WASD move · Mouse / arrows look · E interact / hold to collect · F flashlight · Shift sprint · Ctrl / C crouch · G decoy · M map · J evidence journal · Esc pause. Your current objective always shows the next step. On touch screens, move with the left stick, drag the view to look, use the labeled action buttons, and hold Use / Hide to collect evidence or repair.
Includes jumpscares, a gradual whiteout, and camera shake. You can reduce the effects in the game menu.

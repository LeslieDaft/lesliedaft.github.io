After Bell

A friend dared you to stay until next Monday. Dawn arrives automatically. Miss a night's proof and Jamie makes you stay another day. Take five-question math/science and music theory tests during school: an A earns that teacher’s protection tonight, including a third-person rescue if they are nearby when another watcher catches you. Below 70% sends you to the principal and blocks protection on the next school day. Weekends still have only GoGuardian and fatal captures.
Controls: WASD move · Mouse / arrows look · E interact or take a test · F flashlight · Shift sprint · Ctrl crouch · M map · Esc pause.
Includes jumpscares, a gradual whiteout, and camera shake. You can reduce the effects in the game menu.

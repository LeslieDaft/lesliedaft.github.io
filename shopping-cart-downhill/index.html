Shopping Cart Downhill

Drift three winding courses, launch ramps, take shortcuts, and survive spectacular ragdoll crashes. Unlock carts in the garage.

How to play

A/D or arrows steers; W/up tucks; S/down brakes; Shift drifts; hold Space then release to hop; R restarts; P pauses.

Getting started

Brake before tight turns, then drift through the corner. Hold and release jump to clear obstacles, and use air steering to line up with the road before landing. Collect groceries and bolts to unlock carts.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit Daft Arcade at lesliedaft.github.io and use its games and tools.

Information used by the site

  • Browser saves: Games and tools may store high scores, progress, settings, calculation history, and game-open counts using local storage on your device. Browser storage remains until you clear it or the feature replaces it. Clearing site data can erase saved progress.
  • Technical information: Hosting, advertising, and connected services may receive your IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, and request times to deliver content, secure services, measure advertising, and prevent abuse.
  • Optional online features: Multiplayer features may send session codes, player names you choose, game state, and gameplay messages to the service and other session participants. Do not share sensitive information in player names or public sessions.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

This site includes Google AdSense advertising code. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. Advertising services may also use device identifiers and similar technologies to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud.

You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. Opting out of personalization does not necessarily remove ads.

AI tools and uploaded pictures

When an AI feature is connected and you request help, the text, working, conversation context, or selected picture needed for your request is sent to the configured AI backend and its provider, OpenAI. Do not submit names, contact details, private school records, or other sensitive information. Printed-text recognition in the calculator runs in your browser; downloading its recognition engine involves requests to external services. The AI photo solver sends the selected image when you use that feature.

AI providers and hosting services may retain request information under their own policies. We do not promise that requests are immediately deleted. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

Hosting and other services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Some features load software, fonts, or other resources from external providers; those providers receive the technical information needed to serve the request. External links and connected services have their own privacy practices. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Ad reporting sign-in

The optional Ad Impressions Tracker uses Google sign-in to request read-only access to the authorized user's AdSense reports. It is intended for the site operator. Google handles sign-in; the tracker uses the authorization token to request reports from Google's API. Visitors do not need to sign in to this tracker to play games.

Your choices

  • Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies and clear this site's local storage. Some features and saves may stop working.
  • Use the advertising choices linked above and any privacy or consent controls presented by the advertising service.
  • Choose whether to use online multiplayer or AI features and what information to submit.
  • For data held by an external provider, use that provider's privacy controls or contact process.

Children and personal information

Do not provide personal information through games, player names, or AI prompts. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of online features. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact the site maintainer using the link below; please identify the affected feature without posting the child's personal information publicly.

Questions and requests

Contact the site maintainer through the LeslieDaft GitHub profile. For a general site issue, use the site issue tracker. GitHub issues are public: do not include personal or sensitive information. Browser-only saves can be deleted by clearing this site's data on your device.

Policy updates

This page may change as features and services change. The date above identifies the latest update.

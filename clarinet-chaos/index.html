Squidward: Clarinet Chaos

Blast clarinet notes at SpongeBob and Patrick swarms. Upgrade your solo and survive.

How to play

WASD moves with automatic aiming and firing; Space screeches; E solos; Esc pauses.

Getting started

Keep moving while automatic fire handles nearby enemies. Watch the Screech and Solo readiness indicators and save those abilities for crowded moments.

Wriggle Stack

Rotate worm-shaped pieces, fill complete rows, and keep your stack below the top.

How to play

Left/right moves; up/X rotates; Z reverse rotates; down soft drops; Space hard drops; C holds; P pauses. Touch buttons provide the same actions.

Getting started

Keep your stack flat and leave room for long pieces. Save a useful shape with Hold. Check the landing before hard dropping, which commits immediately.

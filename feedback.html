Feedback & contact

Found a bug or have an idea for a game? Send feedback through the Daft Arcade issue tracker (GitHub sign-in required), or visit the site maintainer’s profile.

Help us reproduce a bug

  • Name the game and the level or mode.
  • Describe what you expected and what happened instead.
  • Include your device, browser and graphics setting.
  • List the steps that trigger the problem. A screenshot can help.

GitHub issues are public. Do not include your real name, school, email, passwords, private photos or other personal information. For privacy requests, do not post the affected personal data publicly.

Common fixes

Click or tap inside the game before using keyboard controls. Escape releases captured mouse controls. For slow 3D games, select School or Performance settings when available. Online rooms can be blocked by restrictive networks. Avoid clearing browser storage unless you are willing to lose local saves.

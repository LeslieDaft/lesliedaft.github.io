Both players keep this page open. Some school or restricted networks may block connections.

How to play

Tap a piece, then a highlighted square. Crimson moves first. Men move and capture diagonally forward; kings move both ways.

Captures are mandatory. Continue every available jump with the same piece. Reaching the far row crowns your piece and ends its turn.

Win by taking every enemy piece or blocking all their moves. Three repeated positions or 80 turns without a capture or promotion is a draw.

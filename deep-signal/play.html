SHIP SYSTEMS

Graphics & controls

Far objects use simple silhouettes; nearby machinery and creatures keep their detail. Shorter distances and lower resolution improve performance.

Command reference

HELM
W/S forward / reverse, A/D turn, Q descend, E ascend, Shift boost. Arrow keys also steer. Space fires your mounted guns.

HYDRO GUNS
Move the mouse to aim, hold left click or Space to fire hydrophobic rounds. Your pilot holds position.

TORPEDOES
Aim at a creature and click or press Space to launch. Tubes reload automatically. Buy a tube at the dock.

MINING
Approach a glowing seam within 36 m of the drill. Hold click or Space to cut ore; your engineer mines more slowly when on auto.

DOCK / UPGRADES
Return to the cyan surface platform. B opens refit. Sell cargo, repair, select a mounting point, then buy and install a part. Research unlocks better equipment.

PRESSURE
Crush depth is a safety limit, not a wall. Going deeper damages the hull. Reinforcing bars, ribs, and plates increase that limit and add weight.

1–4 changes your station. H help. Esc pause. R recovers a disabled submarine at the dock for a percentage of credits and lost cargo. Red sonar blips: creatures. Gold: ore. Cyan: dock.

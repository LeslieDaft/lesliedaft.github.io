SHIP SYSTEMS

Graphics & controls

The three exterior camera feeds render real ocean views at 256 × 144, round-robin at a total of about 10 frames per second. They are never three full-resolution scene renders per game frame.

Inside Nautilus

DIRECT CONTROL
Right-drag the mouse to look around the cabin. Left click any button or screen directly. Touch users drag to look and tap to click. No interaction key or interface-open mode is required.

SIT / STAND / WALK
Click STAND above the dome or on a station screen. WASD / arrows now walk through the cabin. Click a station's SIT button or its seat label to sit there. The three dash monitors show live port, starboard and aft views.

HELM
Click thrust, turn, dive, ascend, level and hold controls on the helm screen. Alternatively use WASD, Q/E and Shift while seated at helm. The AI pilot holds when you're at another station or walking.

WEAPONS / MINING
The left console operates hydrophobic guns, the right launches torpedoes, and the port-side aft screen operates the drill. Click their physical controls. Seated operators can also aim through the window and hold click / Space. The crew operates the others.

ONBOARD FABRICATOR
The table behind the helm on the right has a touchscreen. Choose mount, choose compatible part, then BUY & PLACE. Research prerequisites unlock advanced parts. It works anywhere underwater. Selling cargo and hull repair require the cyan Anchor Station dock.

THE DEEP
Hunt red sonar contacts for bounties. Mine gold / purple wall seams for cargo. Going past the rated crush depth damages the hull. Bars, ribs and plates increase tolerance but add mass. Distant movement, muffled impacts and pressure creaks are warnings.

Esc pause. H help. R or the emergency button recovers a disabled submarine, losing cargo and 15% credits. Lower graphic distances and resolution on slower devices.

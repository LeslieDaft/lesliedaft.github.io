Spider-Man: City Under Siege

Swing across the skyline, explore freely, and face eight supervillains.

How to play

WASD moves; Space jumps/swings; Shift sprints/dives; E zips; F attacks; Q dodges; R webs; C interacts; T returns to a rooftop; Esc pauses.

Getting started

Try Free Roam to learn swinging before entering the campaign. Release your swing to carry momentum and use rooftop recovery if you lose your bearings. Select School graphics before loading on slower devices.

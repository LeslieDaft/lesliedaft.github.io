FIELD MANUAL

Make the bends work for you.

  1. Choose a map. Build on grass beside the road. The highlighted circle shows firing range.
  2. Read the next wave's repeated sequence. Enemies follow the road from the green gate to your blue core; bosses enter last on every fifth wave.
  3. Mix damage types: armor blocks physical damage; elemental attacks and explosions bypass it. Splash clears packs, slows buy time, and snipers punish bosses.
  4. Select built turrets for three paid upgrades and First, Strong, or Fast targeting. Sell returns 70% of all credits invested.
  5. Support auras affect nearby towers and do not stack with copies of the same aura. Income buildings pay when a wave is cleared, and there is no building cap.
  6. Your defense saves between waves and after building during breaks. Continue after wave 30 to keep playing into unlimited waves.

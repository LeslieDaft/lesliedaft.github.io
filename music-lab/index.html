Music playground

KeyJam

Turn your number keys into a band. Play eight instruments, a drum kit, and echo challenges.

How to play

1 through 0, minus and equals play notes. Brackets change octave; Esc stops sound. Enable sound first; pads also accept touch.

Getting started

Enable sound before starting. Practice a short melody on one instrument, then record it. In Band Career, practice runs are slower and a score of 65% unlocks the next event.

Dead Air

You survive a crash on Blackpine Mountain. A ranger on the radio promises to guide you home, but his directions stop matching the world around you. Explore a 1.3 × 1.76 km mountain with logging roads, cabins, two creek crossings, a lookout and an independent route to rescue. Investigate landmarks in any order; the radio is optional.

How to play

WASD and arrow keys both move; mouse looks; Shift sprints using stamina; E interacts with nearby landmarks; hold R to use the radio; M opens the map; F toggles the flashlight; J opens the log; Escape pauses. Click the game for mouse look. On touch, use the movement buttons and drag the scene to look. Space advances subtitles; choose dialogue options with the buttons.

Field notes

Orange posts mark trails; power lines lead toward the south relay. Use the map to find the western ford or eastern service crossing. The radio can locate shelter, explain supplies and suggest directions, but you can navigate without it. Search Team Two is stranded at the fire lookout. Escort Mara to the west gate and hold E to start the escape truck. Medical supplies at camp speed up the escort; fuel at the generator shortens the escape. C crouches. Radio threat scans, decoy transmissions and flares help manage the hunter; calls and sprinting attract it. Repairing the south relay and north generator powers the ridge release, not the rescue gate. Whiteouts drain warmth faster; cabins restore it. Face the hunter with your flashlight to push it back. Crouch beyond 24 meters with the light off to hide. Exploration autosaves. Choose School graphics on slower devices.

Includes unsettling imagery and a frozen body. This edition has subtitles, wind, footsteps and radio effects; spoken dialogue is not included.

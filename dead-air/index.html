Dead Air

You survive a crash on Blackpine Mountain. A ranger on the radio promises to guide you home, but his directions stop matching the world around you. Explore a snowy forest, inspect clues and choose whom to trust across seven chapters and four endings.

How to play

WASD moves; mouse or arrow keys look; Shift runs; E interacts near the current landmark; F toggles the flashlight; J opens the radio log; Escape pauses. Click the game for mouse look. On touch, use the movement buttons and drag the scene to look. Space advances subtitles; choose dialogue options with the buttons.

Field notes

Orange trail markers and the compass point toward your next landmark. Inspect supplies at the hut and the frozen ranger: a flare or spare battery can change your rescue. Contradictions are recorded in the radio log. Checkpoints save after conversations. Choose School graphics before starting on slower devices.

Includes unsettling imagery and a frozen body. This edition has subtitles, wind, footsteps and radio effects; spoken dialogue is not included.

Quidditch: Sky Arena

Choose your rider, chase the Snitch, score through the hoops, and knock rivals back with balls and bats.

How to play

W/S pitches up/down; A/D turns; Space accelerates; Shift boosts; click/F throws or swings; E catches/steals; Q switches rider; P pauses.

Getting started

Tilt toward your destination before accelerating. Switch riders to reach the action and watch which role you control. Use the shorter match setting while learning flight.

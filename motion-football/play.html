Own the pocket.

Play both sides of a four-quarter game. Control offense on your drives and defense against the AI.

Space snaps the ball. W / ↑ moves forward, S / ↓ backs up. A / ← turns left and D / → turns right smoothly. Keep holding W / ↑ to run in the direction you face.

Hold 1–5, then release to pass. Tap = 45°; longer hold = flatter. R throws away.

After the catch, steer the runner. Shift sprints. Pick Inside, Off tackle, or Sweep on the play strip, then press Space for a handoff.

Pick Field goal or Punt to start the kick meter. Press Space to lock power, then again at the center for accuracy. Returns play out live. On AI possessions, control defense: W/S runs, A/D steers, Q/E switches players, Space plays the ball, and F dives to tackle. Earn 10 yards in four downs. A touchdown scores 6: choose an actual 1-point kick or a 2-point pass/run. Finish ahead after four quarters.

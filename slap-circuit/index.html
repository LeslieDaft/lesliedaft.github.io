WELCOME TO THE CIRCUIT

WIN WITH YOUR HEAD.
AND YOUR HAND.

  1. Build your kit. Bring three distinct slaps. Each is usable once every three-turn round. Recovering also uses a turn.
  2. Read the stance. High guard blocks hooks, low guard blocks uppercuts, and counter stance punishes heavy swings. Snap slaps interrupt counters; feints open guards.
  3. Land your timing. Pick a slap, then press Space or Strike when the moving marker is inside gold. The inner strip is a perfect hit. Missing still spends the slap and stamina.
  4. Read the tell, then wait. Rival's right shoulder: step LEFT. Left shoulder: step RIGHT. Both hands overhead: BRACE. Commit only when the border flashes gold. Early moves get punished.
  5. Build momentum. A perfect defense gives a damage bonus on your next slap. Stamina matters: exhausting yourself slows the defense window. Opponents have unique habits and later rivals can feint.
  6. Earn and improve. Buy power, precision, chin, or endurance between bouts. Beat each rival to unlock the next. Your career autosaves; use Export for a portable backup.

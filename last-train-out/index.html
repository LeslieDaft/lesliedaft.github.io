Last Train Out

Rooftop survival on a moving train. Fight the swarm and keep the engine running until dawn.

A/D or arrows: move. Space/W/↑: jump the gaps. J/F: swing your weapon toward the direction you face. Hold E near the front engine to repair. At stations choose 1–3 for supplies or 4 to skip. Stops attract more monsters. Survive 210 seconds until dawn. P pauses, R restarts, M mutes. Best score saves on this device.

Expedition guide

Explore east for distance. Walk as far as you can, or mine coal and operate the train. Enter through the yellow door, load coal at the furnace, then use the engine lever. Most workstations are inside: ammo bench, stove, storage and bed. Climb the ladder to shoot from the roof for extra bounty money. Mine copper to craft ammunition, chop trees for construction, and hunt deer, rabbits and boars. Cook meat at the stove or a campfire. Traders buy materials and sell supplies. Night brings larger zombie packs. Barricade your train or make a camp on the ground.

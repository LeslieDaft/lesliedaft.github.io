Open-world pixel survival

Last Train Out

Explore on foot or fuel your train. Mine coal and copper, hunt, craft bullets, loot guns, and build across a wild frontier.

How to play

WASD/arrows move; mouse aims; click fires; E interacts or mines; Space swings; 1–4 selects weapons; R reloads; F eats; C crafts; T toggles the train aboard; P pauses.

Getting started

Stock coal before committing to a long train journey. Mine copper for ammunition and use the safe train interior to prepare for the more dangerous nights.

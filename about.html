About Daft Arcade

Daft Arcade is Isaac’s collection of browser games and interactive tools, built with AI coding assistance and shaped by player feedback. The project explores everything from sports and rhythm games to vehicle combat, parkour and horror.

Play your way

Open a game from the catalog to find its description and instructions. Control support varies by game; check its listing and help panel. Many games save progress in your browser, so clearing site data can erase your saves.

How the arcade improves

Player suggestions guide changes to controls, performance, difficulty and new features. Some projects are experiments or previews; these are labelled in the catalog. Found something that does not match its description? Please report it.

Feedback and privacy

Report a problem or suggest a feature. Read the privacy policy for browser saves, connected features and advertising information.
